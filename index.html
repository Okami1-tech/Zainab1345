03 — TAWAKKUL • SABR • SHUKR
The prayer I keep making
☾

Ya Allah,
make it beautiful.

Make our path to Nikkah easy and blessed. Guide our decisions, provide what we need through halal means, soften the hearts of our families, and protect us from what is not good for us.

When the road feels long, grant us patience. When doors open, grant us gratitude. And when You unite us, make our home a place of sakinah, mercy and love.

Āmīn, yā Rabbal-‘ālamīn. 🤲

04 — A DU'A, NOT A DEMAND
To the one I keep praying for

Sarauniya ta 💜

Alhamdulillah for Phase 2. I don't take any of these steps for granted. Every little opening reminds me to thank Allah and work even harder.

I cannot promise that every part of the journey will be easy, but I can promise to keep approaching it with sincerity, responsibility and a heart that wants to do things the right way.

I pray that the same Allah who has brought us this far guides every step ahead, makes our preparations sufficient, and allows us to meet at the door of Nikkah with grateful hearts.

For now, let's keep making dua, keep doing our part, and keep saying Alhamdulillah.

With love and prayers,
Muhammad
